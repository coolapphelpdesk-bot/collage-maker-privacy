Privacy Policy

Mosaic: Photos Collage Maker · last updated October 26, 2026

1. Summary

Mosaic: Photos Collage Maker (“the app”) is a photo collage maker published by Cool App Helpdesk. You pick photos, arrange them in a layout, and export the collage as an image.

  • There is no account or sign-in, and we (the developer) never receive any data about you or your photos.
  • Your photos and collages stay on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The app needs no access to your photo gallery. It only sees the photos you choose in Android’s photo picker.
  • The app contains the Google AdMob advertising SDK. It is inactive until September 1, 2027: before that date it is never started, shows no ads and sends nothing. From that date the app shows a small banner on its browsing screens, and Google processes some device data to serve it, as described in section 5.
  • The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

2. Data we collect

The developer collects nothing. We have no server and receive no personal data, photos, usage data or analytics from the app.

From September 1, 2027 the Google AdMob SDK may collect the following to show ads. The developer has no access to it; it is processed by Google:

Your photos, collages and anything you create are never part of this.

3. Data stored on your device

Everything the app keeps is stored in its private storage on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The app opts out of Android cloud backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account either.

Photos are chosen through the Android system photo picker, which grants the app access only to the photos you select; the app never reads the rest of your gallery and requests no media, camera, location, microphone or contacts permission. Exports are saved through Android’s standard media storage. You can also share photos into the app from another app, which gives it access to just those photos.

5. Network activity and third parties

All photo editing and exporting happens on your device. The app’s own features never connect to the internet, and your photos are never transmitted.

Before September 1, 2027: the Google AdMob SDK is bundled but never initialised. No ad request, consent request or any other connection to Google is made, and no ads are shown.

From September 1, 2027: a banner ad is shown at the bottom of the Home, Templates and Projects screens — never while you edit, import or export. Google AdMob may then process the data listed in section 2 to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires your consent for personalised ads (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for it before personalised ads are requested. Google acts as an independent controller for this processing; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no social, attribution or cloud-storage SDK. When you share an exported collage through the system share sheet, the app hands the image to the app you choose; the developer does not see what you share.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the developer collects no personal information from anyone.

7. Data retention and deletion

The developer stores no data off your device. Deleting a collage in the app removes it; photo copies no longer used by any collage are removed automatically. To remove everything, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Mosaic → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Exported images in Pictures/Mosaic are your files and remain until you delete them. Data processed by Google for advertising (from September 1, 2027) is retained under Google’s policies linked in section 5.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing, and is excluded from Android cloud backup. Connections made by the ad SDK use encryption (HTTPS). The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
